# Scanned lead

An attendee an organization's staff scanned at the booth.

## Information kept

- **Attendee** — the attendee's pseudonymous badge identifier
- **Scanned at** — when it was scanned
- **Scan type** — how it was scanned
- **Device name** — the device that scanned it
- **Booth ID** — the booth at the time of the scan
- **Booth name** — the booth's name at the time of the scan
- **Attendee data** — the attendee details the event shares with exhibitors, taken at the time of the scan
- **Note** — the staff's note
- **Tags** — the staff's tags
